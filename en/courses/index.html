Courses

MR CHINESE courses are built on the standard HSK textbooks and curriculum, covering listening, speaking, reading, writing, correct pronunciation, pinyin, Chinese characters, sentence building and practical conversation — together with Chinese culture and communication style.

Session length
75 minutes
Group class size
5–7 learners
Format
In person, in Zanjan
Class times
Mostly afternoons and evenings

The exact weekly schedule may change each term. To learn about the schedule, capacity and tuition of the new term, request a consultation.

The MR CHINESE teaching method

The main resource is the standard HSK textbook series — but the teaching does not stop at the book.

Skills we work on

  • Listening
  • Speaking
  • Reading
  • Writing
  • Correct pronunciation
  • Pinyin
  • Recognizing and writing Chinese characters
  • Sentence building
  • Practical conversation
  • Chinese culture and communication style

Supporting tools and methods

  • Teaching slides adapted into Persian
  • Pronunciation and pinyin practice
  • Sentence building in real situations
  • Everyday, practical conversations
  • Educational games
  • Group activities
  • Q&A
  • Listening practice
  • End-of-term test and assessment
  • Learning support outside class
  • Continuous homework and exercises

Classroom atmosphere

Classes are not meant to be dry and one-way. Learners actively participate in exercises, sentence building, conversation, games and group activities. The goal is a class that is lively, friendly and fun — and at the same time organized and educational.

Whether you want to learn Chinese out of personal interest, or for study, migration, business, translation or teaching — share your goal with us first, and we will recommend the right starting path for you.
