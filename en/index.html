What is MR CHINESE?

MR CHINESE is an independent educational brand for learning Chinese, focused on the standard HSK curriculum, conversation, pronunciation, pinyin, Chinese characters, and understanding Chinese culture.

Here, Chinese is not just a school subject — it is a tool for real connection, cultural understanding, further study, migration, trade, translation, teaching, and building new opportunities.

Who is learning Chinese for?

Our learners start this journey with different goals:

  • Personal interest in Chinese language and culture
  • Further education
  • Migration
  • Business and communication with Chinese companies or partners
  • Entering the field of translation
  • Becoming a Chinese language teacher
  • Building international connections
  • Using Chinese in a future career

No prior Chinese is needed to start HSK 1 — the only prerequisite is familiarity with the English alphabet. Our current learners range from 15 to 51 years old.

Why MR CHINESE?

What makes this learning path different:

01

Direct, everyday teacher support

Throughout the course, you have direct access to the teacher in private messages for your questions and learning difficulties.

02

Semi-private classes

Group classes usually have 5–7 learners, so the teacher can closely follow each learner's pronunciation, conversation, participation and progress.

03

Active, energetic classes

Games, group practice, conversation, Q&A and interactive activities keep learning far from dry, one-way lecturing.

04

Based on the standard HSK curriculum

You follow a clear, step-by-step learning path built on standard HSK materials.

05

Beyond the textbook

Alongside the book, you learn practical expressions, real conversation situations, and insights into Chinese culture and communication style.

06

Connecting with Chinese friends

Depending on your level and when circumstances allow, opportunities are created for conversation and cultural exchange with Chinese friends.

07

Guidance on China-related paths

You can receive guidance and shared experience about migration and study, business with China, translation, and the path to becoming a teacher — as general advice, not legal services or guaranteed outcomes.

08

A path into teaching

Learners who complete the courses with strong performance, consistency and communication skills can, after evaluation, prepare to join MR CHINESE as teachers. This is not a guarantee of employment — it depends on ability, commitment, capacity and final approval.
