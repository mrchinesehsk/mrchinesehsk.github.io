Alongside learning and teaching Chinese, Alireza has ongoing, first-hand experience communicating with Chinese friends and business partners.

Before founding MR CHINESE, he worked in stone exports and business communication with China. That experience taught him to see Chinese not merely as a school subject, but as a tool for real connection, trade, cultural understanding and new opportunities.

His goal is not simply to finish textbooks — he wants learners to actually use what they learn in their lives, relationships and professional future.

«Education is valuable when it creates new opportunities, abilities and connections in people's lives.»

How MR CHINESE began

MR CHINESE was founded on April 19, 2026 (30 Farvardin 1405) — but its story began slightly earlier.

During a difficult period of Alireza's life, his main work in stone exports came to a halt, and for a while his usual working relationship with his Chinese business partner was interrupted. In those circumstances, one question took shape: “How can I build a new, value-creating path out of the skills and experience I already have?”

He was practicing and reviewing Chinese daily at the time, and the answer became a practical decision: to start teaching Chinese privately. That same night he began searching for his first student — and found them in Zanjan.

It soon became clear that more people were interested in learning Chinese, but access to specialized Chinese classes in Zanjan was limited. The first session was held with two learners in a café; once enrollments were confirmed, classes moved to a professional teaching space.

From the very beginning, MR CHINESE has operated under its own name, identity and teaching method — and is now building a community of people interested in the Chinese language, culture and the opportunities connected to China.

Assessment and certificates

At the end of each term, learners are assessed based on criteria such as attendance, participation, homework and the test result.

Progress certificate

Issued after completing the intermediate terms of each level.

MR CHINESE course completion certificate

Issued after completing each book, together with the assessment result and score.

These certificates are issued by MR CHINESE and are not official government documents or international HSK exam certificates. The official HSK exam has its own independent registration, examination and certification process — and if you wish, you will be guided in preparing for it.

Whether you want to learn Chinese out of personal interest, or for study, migration, business, translation or teaching — share your goal with us first, and we will recommend the right starting path for you.
